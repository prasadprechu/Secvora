## Introduction

Modern AI applications rarely rely on a single component.

They may use third-party models, open-source libraries, datasets, model repositories, APIs, plugins, and other external dependencies.

This interconnected ecosystem creates an AI model supply chain that must be secured throughout the development and deployment lifecycle.

## What Is AI Model Supply Chain Security?

AI model supply chain security focuses on protecting the components and dependencies used to build, train, deploy, and operate AI systems.

These components may include:

- Pre-trained models
- Training datasets
- Open-source libraries
- Model dependencies
- AI development tools
- External APIs
- Model repositories

## Key Supply Chain Risks

### Malicious or Compromised Models

A model obtained from an untrusted source may contain unexpected behavior or security weaknesses.

Organizations should evaluate the provenance and integrity of models before deployment.

### Compromised Dependencies

AI applications often depend on third-party packages and frameworks.

A vulnerable or compromised dependency can introduce risk into an otherwise secure application.

### Dataset Risks

Training and retrieval data can influence AI system behavior.

Untrusted or manipulated datasets can introduce data quality and security concerns.

## Securing the AI Supply Chain

Organizations can improve security by implementing:

- Trusted component sources
- Dependency monitoring
- Model provenance tracking
- Integrity verification
- Security testing
- Access controls
- Continuous monitoring

## Why AI Supply Chain Security Matters

AI applications increasingly depend on components maintained outside an organization's direct control.

Understanding where models, datasets, libraries, and services originate can help security teams identify risks before they reach production.

## Conclusion

AI model supply chain security is an important part of modern AI security.

Organizations should evaluate the provenance, integrity, dependencies, and behavior of AI components throughout the development and deployment lifecycle.
